Questions & answers.

Yes, SwachhLens is 100% free for citizens. There are no fees, subscriptions, or hidden charges. The platform is built for municipal use and funded by the city — citizens simply download, register, and start reporting waste in their area.
When a citizen submits a report, the AI analyses the location, waste type, and severity to suggest the best area group and crew member. A group lead then reviews and approves (or overrides) the assignment. This ensures the right team handles the right job — fast.
Absolutely. Every report moves through a transparent pipeline: PENDING → IN_PROGRESS → VERIFY → RESOLVED. You'll see real-time status updates on your dashboard as the crew is dispatched, arrives, and completes the cleanup.
Crews upload a photo of the cleaned area when they mark a job as collected. This photo, along with location data, is reviewed by the group lead before the report is marked as RESOLVED. This verification step ensures quality and accountability.
SwachhLens is designed for Indian municipal corporations (Nagar Nigam). It can be deployed in any city with a waste management crew structure. The system is configurable for different ward boundaries, crew sizes, and area group layouts.
