FAQs

Questions & answers.

Is SwachhLens really free for citizens?

Yes. SwachhLens is 100% free for citizens, crew members, and municipalities. There are no hidden fees, no subscriptions, and no paywalls. Report as much waste as you want.

How does the AI dispatch work?

When a citizen submits a photo report, the AI analyses the waste type, location, and urgency — then suggests the best crew and zone. A group lead reviews and approves before any dispatch happens.

Can I track my report after submitting?

Absolutely. Every report goes through a visible pipeline: submitted → AI prioritised → dispatched → in progress → verified clean. You get notified at every step.

What proof do crews upload?

Crews upload before-and-after photos of each cleanup. These are stored permanently and visible to citizens, group leads, and municipal administrators.

Which cities can use SwachhLens?

SwachhLens is built for Indian Nagar Nigam municipalities. It's currently live in pilot wards and expanding. Any city with a municipal sanitation department can get started.
