About SwachhLens

An AI-powered civic waste reporting and cleanup verification platform helping citizens and municipal teams build cleaner, smarter cities.

What is SwachhLens?

SwachhLens is a citizen-first waste-management platform designed for Indian municipalities. It connects citizens who spot waste issues with municipal cleaning crews through an AI-powered dispatch and verification system.

Every report enters a single pipeline — from the citizen's photo to a verified, logged cleanup. Nothing falls through because every step has an owner.

The Problem We Solve

In cities across India, overflowing bins, illegal dumping, and missed pickups are everyday frustrations. Citizens often don't know how to report these issues, and municipal teams lack visibility into where cleanup is needed most.

How It Works

  • Citizen Reports: Citizens snap a photo of waste, and SwachhLens routes it to the right cleaning crew.
  • AI Waste Detection: Our AI analyzes photos to classify waste type and severity, helping prioritize dispatches.
  • Municipal Dispatch: AI suggests crew assignments, but a group lead approves every dispatch — ensuring human oversight.
  • Cleanup Crew: Cleaning crews receive tasks, clear the waste, and upload proof of completed work.
  • Verification: Group leads verify each cleanup before it's marked resolved — creating a complete accountability loop.

Our Mission

We believe every citizen should be able to report waste in seconds, and every report should lead to verified action. SwachhLens turns citizen participation into measurable urban improvement — one civic loop at a time.

Smart City Initiative

SwachhLens is built as part of the broader smart city vision — using technology to make urban governance more transparent, responsive, and effective. By connecting citizens directly to municipal services through AI, we're helping cities become cleaner and more accountable.
